Found it Monday.
Lost it by Friday.
The record survives. Everything attached to it does not.
Between one session and the next, the four things she needed
in order to decide come loose from the thing they belonged to
— and what is left is a title she can no longer act on.
The redesign attaches them permanently: identity in the seal, terms in
one structure, the deadline on the card’s own edge, the commitment
dated at the moment it is made.

The
opportunity
card.
One object has to carry an entire category of
decision: who is offering this, what it asks of
you, when it stops being available, and
whether you already claimed it.
I T M U S T C A R R Y
Organization identity
Category and type
Role title
Location
Commitment and duration
Availability
Deadline
Save state and its date
A N D M U S T N O T B E C O M E
A listing tile. A property card. A course thumbnail.
A ticker row. Anything whose grammar was
borrowed from a market that is not this one.
RING SEAL
Open ring, keyed at this category's clock
position. Verified partners carry a second
ring.
TITLE, TWO LINES MAX
Never italic. Role titles are product data, not
editorial voice.
ONE TERMS LINE
Commitment, then duration, start and
places. Not four KPI tiles.
SAVE CONTROL
Ring becomes filled seal: fill + glyph + label
+ date — never colour alone.
DEADLINE LEAVES THE OUTLINE
A real closing date grows a tab on the
card’s own edge. “Rolling” grows nothing.
THE DATED RECORD
Saved at 9:41 on Tuesday. The receipt is part
of the object, not a toast that passed.
CATEGORY RULE
3px of the category’s identity colour — the
only chrome the plate carries.
